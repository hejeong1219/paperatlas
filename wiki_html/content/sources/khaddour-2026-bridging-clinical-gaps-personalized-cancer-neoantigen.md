# Bridging clinical gaps in personalized cancer neoantigen vaccines

_Cancer Cell, 2026._ [10.1016/j.ccell.2026.04.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2026.04.002) · [PubMed 42114514](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42114514/)

## Summary

이 Cancer Cell Review(Dana-Farber Khaddour·Ott)는 personalized cancer neoantigen vaccine(PCV)의 임상 적용 격차를 진단·해결 관점에서 정리합니다. NGS·bioinformatics 진보로 neoantigen 발굴이 standardized되고 vaccine platform(peptide·mRNA·DNA·viral vector·DC)이 다양화된 현 상황에서, PCV가 적합한 tumor 적응증(pancreatic·MSS CRC·prostate처럼 ICI refractory cancer, ICI-treated 환자의 sub-group, adjuvant/perioperative 세팅 등), ICI/chemotherapy/RT/CAR-T 등과의 병용 timing·sequencing, 그리고 long-term durability 보고가 부족한 핵심 translational gap을 분석합니다. monovalent shared neoantigen(KRAS 등) vs polyvalent personalized 디자인 비교와 함께 adjuvant 세팅에서 minimal residual disease 환자에게 PCV가 가장 큰 benefit을 줄 가능성을 시사합니다.

## Key Points

- PCV는 NGS·bioinformatics 기반 개별 환자 mutation에서 neoantigen을 식별·인코딩하여 tumor-specific T cell repertoire를 broaden, ICI에 substrate를 제공하고 central tolerance를 우회한다는 4가지 이론적 장점을 정리한다.
- 적응증: ICI 효능이 낮은 pancreatic cancer, MSS CRC, prostate cancer 등 cold tumor와, ICI-treated 환자의 minimal residual disease/adjuvant 세팅이 PCV의 가장 유망한 임상 영역으로 제시된다.
- 병용 전략: ICI, chemotherapy, RT, CAR-T, oncolytic virus와의 결합에서 timing·sequencing·synergistic potential을 신중히 고려해야 하며, neoadjuvant–surgery–adjuvant 세팅에 따라 vaccine 도입 시점이 달라진다.
- 디자인 비교: monovalent shared neoantigen(KRAS·TP53 hotspot) vs polyvalent personalized 디자인의 장단점과 platform(peptide·mRNA·DNA·viral vector·DC) 선택 가이드를 제시한다.
- translational gap: long-term durability, T cell quality·persistence, biomarker integration, 그리고 vaccine 효능을 어떤 환자에 어떤 시점에 적용할지에 대한 stratification 부재가 핵심 미해결 과제로 지적된다.

## Abstract (PubMed)

Personalized cancer neoantigen vaccines (PCV) represent an individualized form of immunotherapy. Neoantigens encoded by individual tumor mutations offer precise targets for robust, tumor-specific immunity. Next-generation sequencing and advanced bioinformatics have facilitated personalized neoantigen discovery, while progress in vaccine platforms has enabled optimization of delivery technologies and generated a versatile and scalable framework for neoantigen vaccine therapy, allowing therapeutic administration across diverse clinical settings. The integration of neoantigen vaccines with other treatment modalities requires careful consideration of timing, sequencing, and synergistic potential. In this review, we synthesize emerging data highlighting tumor indications suitable to investigate PCVs, define optimal clinical settings for vaccine administration, and discuss combinatorial regimens to enhance efficacy, as well as the timing and sequencing in multimodal treatment, aiming to address key translational opportunities and challenges essential to deploying neoantigen vaccines broadly in the clinic.
