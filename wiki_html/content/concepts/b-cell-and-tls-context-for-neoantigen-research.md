# B-Cell and TLS Context for Neoantigen Research

This axis asks how B cells, plasma cells, TLS maturity, and spatial immune organization shape the success or failure of neoantigen-directed immunity.

## Key Points

- B-cell/TLS biology is not only a “correlate of response” but can be a mechanistic gate between neoantigen presence and effective tumor killing (local antigen presentation, T-cell help, DC licensing, and durable memory).
- When TLS are mature, they may increase the probability that a given neoantigen intervention yields a productive local immune circuit (especially when CD4 help and B-cell differentiation are intact).
- B cells can plausibly support neoantigen responses through multiple routes that should be kept distinct rather than blended:
  - **Antigen presentation**: B cells present antigen to CD4 T cells (context-dependent; quality and specificity matter).
  - **Tfh and germinal-center-like programs**: TLS maturity is often read out as GC-like structures and Tfh states that enable affinity maturation and durable immune organization.
  - **DC cross-priming support**: antibody/immune-complex capture and Fc-mediated uptake can change antigen routing, potentially impacting cross-presentation.
  - **Antibody and immune-complex effects**: can enhance opsonization and uptake, but can also be immunosuppressive depending on Fc context and myeloid state.
- Failure modes should be framed explicitly: strong neoantigen candidates can still fail if immune architecture is “incomplete” (immature TLS, lack of Tfh help, suppressive myeloid compartment, poor spatial access).

## Practical Reading Checklist (for TLS papers)

- What is the **TLS maturity definition** (GC-like markers, spatial organization, cell-state signatures)?
- Which **B-cell states** are emphasized (naive, memory, GC B, plasma) and how are they measured (IHC, scRNA, spatial)?
- What is the hypothesized **causal direction** (TLS drives response vs response/treatment induces TLS)?
- Are results tied to a specific **therapy context** (ICI, chemo-ICI, radiation, vaccines, adoptive cell therapy)?
- Do authors provide evidence beyond association (perturbation, longitudinal sampling, functional assays)?

## Key Questions

- When do B cells and TLS improve antitumor immunity?
- Which TLS features matter most for response?
- Can strong neoantigen candidates fail because the local immune architecture is weak?
