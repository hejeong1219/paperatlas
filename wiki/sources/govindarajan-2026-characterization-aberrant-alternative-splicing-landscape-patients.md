# Characterization of aberrant alternative splicing landscape in patients with metastatic renal cell carcinoma

_J Immunother Cancer, 2026._ [10.1016/j.immuni.2022.02.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2022.02.001) · [PubMed 42086309](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42086309/) · [PMC PMC10801831](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10801831/)

## Summary

본 연구는 metastatic renal cell carcinoma(mRCC) 101명 환자의 RNA-seq 데이터에서 aberrant alternative splicing(AS) event를 ICI 및 targeted therapy 반응 군 간 비교 분석하고, novel AS event 유래 peptide의 MHC class I binding 가능성을 평가하여 neoantigen 잠재성을 탐색했습니다. Outlier splicing 분석으로 10개 mRCC-specific aberrant splice event를 식별했고, ICI cohort에서 461개·targeted therapy cohort에서 253개의 differentially spliced event가 responder vs non-responder 사이에서 발견되었으며 intron retention이 우세한 모티프였습니다. 13개 AS event(PTPN6, ACTN1 포함)가 responder에서 enriched되었고 IFFO1·ZNF692 유래 AS peptide가 높은 MHC class I binding potential을 보여, splice burden이 높을수록 antigen processing·adaptive immune 반응이 풍부한 immunogenic TME와 연관되었습니다.

## Key Points

- mRCC 환자 101명의 RNA-seq retrospective 분석에서 outlier splicing 기법으로 10개 mRCC-specific aberrant splice event를 식별했다.
- ICI cohort에서 461개, targeted therapy cohort에서 253개의 differentially spliced event가 responder vs non-responder 사이에서 발견되었고 intron retention이 dominant motif였다.
- responder에서 enriched된 13개 unique AS event 중 PTPN6, ACTN1이 포함되어 면역 신호 관련 splice variant가 반응성과 연관됨을 시사한다.
- IFFO1과 ZNF692 유래 AS peptide가 높은 MHC class I binding potential을 보여 splice-derived neoantigen 후보로 제시되었다.
- 높은 splice burden은 antigen processing 및 adaptive immune response 관련 immunogenic TME와 연관되어, alternative splicing이 mRCC immunotherapy 반응 biomarker·neoantigen source로 활용될 가능성을 보였다.

## Abstract (PubMed)

**PURPOSE**: Aberrant alternative splicing (AS) events have been implicated in cancer progression; however, their role in metastatic renal cell carcinoma (mRCC) remains underexplored. This study aims to identify AS events associated with clinical benefits from immune checkpoint inhibitors and targeted therapies in mRCC.

**MATERIALS AND METHODS**: We conducted a retrospective analysis on 101 patients with mRCC who received systemic therapy and underwent RNA sequencing. Patients were divided into subgroups based on ICIs (alone or in combination) and targeted therapies. Responders and non-responders were classified according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors V.1.1 criteria. Differential gene expression and splicing analyses were performed between responders and non-responders in each cohort. Novel AS events were analyzed for their potential to generate peptide neoantigens through major histocompatibility complex (MHC) class I binding predictions.

**RESULTS**: Outlier splicing analysis identified 10 aberrant splice events specific to mRCC. AS analysis revealed 461 differentially spliced events between responders and non-responders in the ICI cohort and 253 in the targeted therapy cohort, with intron retention as the predominant motif. Thirteen unique AS events were enriched in responders, including PTPN6 and ACTN1. Predictive neoantigen analysis identified high MHC class I binding potential in peptides from AS events in IFFO1 and ZNF692. High splice burden was linked to an immunogenic tumor microenvironment, characterized by enriched antigen processing and adaptive immune responses.

**CONCLUSIONS**: This study provides a comprehensive analysis of AS events in mRCC, highlighting intron retention as potential biomarkers for treatment response. Identified AS-derived neoantigens may serve as potential targets for adoptive cell therapy strategies.
