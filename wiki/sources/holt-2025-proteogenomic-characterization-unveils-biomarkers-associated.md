# Proteogenomic characterization unveils biomarkers associated with chemoresistance in muscle-invasive bladder cancer.

_Cell reports. Medicine, 2025._ PMID: [40749681](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40749681/).

DOI: [10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.102255](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.102255)

## Summary

To explore potential chemoresistance mechanisms and identify therapeutic opportunities in muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), we conduct comprehensive proteogenomic characterization of 46 pre- and 14 post-treatment MIBC tumors incorporating genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and phosphoproteomics. Multi-omics clustering not only recapitulated established molecular subtypes but also revealed subtypes associated with chemotherapy sensitivity. Protein isoform level analysis identifies protein abundance of a short isoform of ATAD1 and RAF family proteins as biomarkers of chemosensitivity. Integration of proteomic and phosphoproteomic data reveals Wnt signaling via GSK3B-S9 phosphorylation and the JAK/STAT pathway as potential targets to overcome chemoresistance. Correlations between PD-L1 and TROP-2/NECTIN-4 indicate an additive benefit of combination therapy targeting these proteins. Overall, this study serves as a valuable resource for researchers and clinicians aiming to better understand and treat chemoresistant MIBC.

## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

- Cohort/scope: 46 pretreatment and 14 posttreatment muscle-invasive bladder cancer tumors profiled with genomics, transcriptomics, TMT proteomics, and TMT phosphoproteomics.
- Proteome: 11,596 gene products identified from 206,856 peptides; downstream data include protein and SEPEP-level quantification.
- Phosphoproteome: 11,799 phosphosites quantified (9,143 serine, 2,266 threonine, and 390 tyrosine sites).
- Acetylome: not reported as a separate acetylome layer in the local PDF.
- MS method: TMT11 quantitative proteomics and phosphoproteomics; high-pH reversed-phase peptide fractionation; Fe-NTA IMAC phosphopeptide enrichment; Philosopher database searching and quantification.
- Instrument/platform: Thermo Fisher EASY-nanoLC coupled to Orbitrap Fusion Lumos Tribrid mass spectrometer.
- Extraction evidence: local PDF Results reports 11,596 gene products and 11,799 quantified phosphosites; Methods report TMT11 labeling, Fe-NTA IMAC enrichment, Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, and Philosopher v4.2.1.
- Interpretation note: the phosphoproteome value is reported as quantified phosphosites, while proteome value is gene products identified.

## Key Points

- The study connects MIBC chemotherapy sensitivity/resistance with proteomic and phosphoproteomic features.
- Protein isoform/SEPEP analysis identifies ATAD1 and RAF-family peptide features associated with chemosensitivity.
- The atlas row should be classified as a tumor cohort with proteome and phosphoproteome only.
