# Proteogenomic characterization of primary colorectal cancer and metastatic progression identifies proteome-based subtypes and signatures

_Cell Reports, 2024._ PMID: [38377004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38377004/).

DOI: [10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113810](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113810)

## Summary

Large primary-versus-metastatic colorectal-cancer (CRC) proteogenomics study that defines proteome-based subtypes for primary CRC and liver metastases, identifies progression-associated hypoxia and stemness signatures, and frames immune-cold metastatic disease via suppression of antigen presentation pathways.

## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

- Cohort/scope: 154 primary CRCs, 142 liver metastatic CRCs, 78 normal colon tissues, and 14 normal liver tissues (as reported in the main PDF text).
- Genomics: whole genomes + transcriptomes + proteomes for 16 patient-matched triplets (mCRC, pCRC, benign colonic mucosa), followed by cohort-scale deep proteome sequencing; many samples also annotated with MSK-IMPACT targeted sequencing.
- Proteome: deep proteome sequencing reports 340 tissue samples (most with matched genomics annotations).
- TME/immune: tumor microenvironment analysis focusing on immune-cold programs and antigen presentation (MHC class I/II, antigen processing).

## Key Points

- Proteomics uncovers distinct subtypes in primary and metastatic CRC (three each), organized around three functional signatures: hypoxia, stemness (including ALT phenotype), and immune programs.
- Metastases enrich for hypoxia-associated metabolic reprogramming and for a stemness program with ALT features (as described in the main PDF text).
- Immune-cold states show suppression of antigen presentation machinery, especially in metastases, providing an “immune evasion during progression” framing.

## Cancer Multiomics Project Relevance

- “원발암–전이암” 진행 축에서 **genome → proteome functional state**가 어떻게 재배선되는지(특히 hypoxia/stemness/immune-cold 축)를 예시로 제공한다.
- Cancer Multiomics 과제에서 WGS 신호(예: SV/CNA/WGD 등)와 phosphoproteome/면역 feature를 연결할 때, “진행/전이에서 항원제시 억제” 같은 면역회피 feature를 결과 변수/설명 변수로 두는 설계를 뒷받침한다.

## Open Questions

- Cohort-scale에서 phosphoproteome/acetylome 같은 PTM 레이어가 포함되는지 여부는 Methods/보충자료 확인이 필요(현 extract 범위에서는 proteome 중심으로 드러남).
