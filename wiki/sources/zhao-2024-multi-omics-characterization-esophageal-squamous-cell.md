# Multi-omics characterization of esophageal squamous cell carcinoma identifies molecular subtypes and therapeutic targets.

_JCI insight, 2024._ PMID: [38315294](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38315294/).

DOI: [10.1172/jci.insight.171916](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.171916)

## Summary

This ESCC cohort study integrates transcriptomics, proteomics, and phosphoproteomics from paired treatment-naive tumors and adjacent tissues to define molecular subtypes and kinase-linked therapeutic hypotheses.

## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

- Cohort/scope: 60 paired treatment-naive esophageal squamous cell carcinoma and adjacent nontumor tissues.
- Proteome: TMT proteomics identified 100,898 nonphosphorylated peptides mapping to 9,261 proteins; 8,224 proteins were quantified for tumor versus adjacent nontumor analysis.
- Phosphoproteome: 28,853 phosphopeptides identified; 18,944 phosphosites on 5,213 phosphoproteins identified with confident localization; 3,368 phosphoproteins were quantified after normalization.
- Acetylome: not reported as a separate acetylome layer in the local PDF.
- MS method: TMT-based isobaric labeling for relative quantification; LC-MS/MS proteomics and phosphoproteomics; MaxQuant v1.6.1.0 phosphoproteomics processing; confident phosphopeptides at 1% FDR and 75% localization probability.
- Instrument/platform: the main PDF states LC-MS/MS and defers detailed LC-MS/MS settings to supplemental methods; exact instrument model was not exposed in the extracted main PDF text.
- Extraction evidence: local PDF Results report 100,898 nonphosphorylated peptides, 28,853 phosphopeptides, 9,261 proteins, 18,944 phosphosites, 5,213 phosphoproteins, and 8,224 quantified proteins.
- Interpretation note: use 9,261 proteins and 18,944 phosphosites for identification-scale visualization; show 8,224 proteins and 3,368 phosphoproteins as filtered quantified features.

## Key Points

- Proteomic clustering identifies three ESCC subtypes with different prognosis and molecular features.
- Phosphoproteomics nominates candidate kinases and supports kinase-substrate network analysis.
- This is a patient tumor cohort with proteome and phosphoproteome layers, but no acetylome layer.
