# Suberoylanilide Hydroxamic Acid (SAHA) Treatment Reveals Crosstalk Among Proteome, Phosphoproteome, and Acetylome in Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma Cells.

_Frontiers in genetics, 2022._ PMID: [35591851](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35591851/).

DOI: [10.3389/fgene.2022.873840](https://doi.org/10.3389/fgene.2022.873840)

## Summary

This study profiles SAHA-treated nasopharyngeal carcinoma 5-8F cells across global proteome, phosphoproteome, and acetylome layers to examine phosphorylation/acetylation crosstalk after HDAC inhibition.

## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

- Cohort/scope: SAHA perturbation in nasopharyngeal carcinoma 5-8F cells; included as a cell-line perturbation rather than a patient tumor cohort.
- Proteome: abstract reports 6,491 proteins detected and quantified; Results text later reports 6,391 quantified proteins, so the source contains a small internal count discrepancy.
- Phosphoproteome: abstract reports 2,456 phosphorylated proteins; the extracted text did not expose a single total phosphosite count.
- Acetylome: 441 lysine acetylation sites on 298 proteins identified; 333 acetylation sites on 228 proteins quantified.
- MS method: TMT 6-plex labeling; TiO2 phosphopeptide enrichment; anti-lysine-acetylation antibody enrichment; high-resolution LC-MS/MS; site probability cutoff 0.75 for phosphorylated and acetylated sites.
- Instrument/platform: Q Exactive quadrupole-Orbitrap mass spectrometer.
- Extraction evidence: local PDF abstract and Methods report 6,491 proteins, 2,456 phosphorylated proteins, 228 acetylated proteins, TMT labeling, TiO2 enrichment, acetyl-lysine antibody enrichment, LC-MS/MS, and Q Exactive.
- Interpretation note: count units differ from cohort atlas papers because phosphoproteome is reported as phosphorylated proteins, while acetylome is reported both as sites and proteins.

## Key Points

- SAHA affects not only acetylation but also phosphorylation in NPC cells.
- The study explicitly includes the three requested layers: proteome, phosphoproteome, and acetylome.
- Because it is a single cell-line perturbation study, it should be visually separated from patient tumor cohorts.
