  - Abstract Background/Aim: Cabozantinib is a multi-target tyrosine kinase inhibitor used in renal cell carcinoma (RCC), yet the exposure-dependent remodeling of phosphorylation networks under short-term versus chronic treatment remains insufficiently defined.
  - This study applied quantitative phosphoproteomics to delineate remodeling programs induced by acute and chronic cabozantinib exposure and to examine cellular features within the same signaling background.
