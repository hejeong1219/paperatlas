# Integrative phosphoproteomic network analysis identifies CAMK2D as a shared regulator of TPD52 family proteins in cancer

## Summary

This paper aggregates thousands of public phosphoproteomics datasets to map phosphorylation patterns of the TPD52 protein family (TPD52 / TPD52L1 / TPD52L2) and to infer shared upstream kinases and co-phosphoregulated signaling partners. It highlights recurrent phosphosites and nominates CAMK2D (along with other kinases) as a putative shared regulator, while also describing cross-cancer phosphosite changes using CPTAC-derived resources.

## Key Points

- Dataset integration: integrates >3,825 public human phosphoproteomic datasets to assemble phosphosite occurrence and differential patterns for TPD52 family proteins.
- Recurrent sites: highlights dominant sites such as TPD52 S171/S176, TPD52L1 S149, and TPD52L2 S12/S166 in conserved domains (as described in the PDF Abstract/Results framing).
- Kinase nomination: identifies CAMK2D as a predominant shared kinase candidate; also discusses other candidate kinases (e.g., CDK2, GRK5) in the shared-regulator set.
- Network context: uses co-phosphoregulation and interaction/network views to connect TPD52-family phosphorylation states to broader signaling programs (e.g., trafficking, cell cycle, cytoskeleton/EMT-related hypotheses).
- Cancer comparisons: reports cancer-type-specific phosphosite shifts (e.g., hypo-/hyper-phosphorylation patterns across cancers) using CPTAC-derived comparison resources (as stated in the PDF).

## Cancer Multiomics Project Relevance

- Cancer Multiomics 과제에서 phosphoproteome 결과를 “개별 phosphosite”가 아니라 **network-level feature/kinase 가설**로 요약하려 할 때, public phosphoproteomics 메타-분석으로 upstream kinase 후보를 만드는 한 가지 레퍼런스가 된다.
- 다만 본 논문은 cohort WGS-연계/치료반응 라벨을 직접 다루기보다는, 공개 phosphoproteomics를 활용한 *signaling map* 성격이 강하므로 Cancer Multiomics 100편 코퍼스 내 우선순위는 낮을 수 있다.
