## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

This section was added for the interactive atlas on recent multi-omics proteomics/PTM identification scale. Values are taken from the local PDF and preserve the paper's own reporting unit.

- Cohort/scope: 95 tumors and 49 normal tissue samples.
- Proteome: 12,153 (identified protein groups).
- Phosphoproteome: 73,212 (phosphosites).
- Acetylome: 10,862 (lysine acetylation sites).
- MS method: TMT-10; bRPLC fractionation; IMAC phosphopeptide enrichment; acetyl-lysine enrichment.
- Instrument/platform: nanoACQUITY UPLC coupled to Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.
- Extraction evidence: STAR Methods: 12,153 proteins, 73,212 phosphosites, and 10,862 lysine acetylation sites across all tumors.
- Interpretation note: Average per tumor was also reported: 10,088 proteins, 29,710 phosphosites, and 3,821 acetylation sites.
