# Integrative proteogenomics maps multifactorial aetiology, progression and therapeutic vulnerabilities in gastric cancer

## Summary

Chang et al. (Gut 2026;75:886–904, BMJ proprietary, doi:10.1136/gutjnl-2025-337247) report a Taiwan-led multi-omic atlas of **154 treatment-naïve East Asian Han Chinese gastric cancer patients** (NTUH + KMUH, 2022-03 to 2024-12, median age 67, 62% male) integrating **WES (14,134 mut genes) + RNA-seq (19,504 transcripts) + TMT proteome (>10,000 protein groups) + phosphoproteome (30,000 phosphosites) + microbiome (RNA + protein) + IHC** with refined anatomical mapping, HP status (HP_E 69.5%, HP_N 30.5%, HP_A 22.7% culture+CagA+) and 7 carcinogen mutational signatures (G1–G7). The work defines **6 carcinogen clusters (C1–C6)** and identifies the **polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon (PAH) signature dibenz[a,h]acridine (DBAC)** as a high-risk East-Asia–specific exposure (HR 2.36–3.16 multivariable Cox; diffuse subtype 5.9× EFS risk; Treg p=0.002), absent in TCGA but present in Taiwanese + Japanese cohorts. BaP cell-line invasion (MKN45 3.7×, MKN28 2.7×) functionally validates PAH-driven proinvasive signalling. Microbial RNA + protein profiling defines **4 microbiome clusters** (HP-dominant, Microvirga-dominant ↔ DBAC p=9.3×10⁻¹², Streptococcus-dominant ↔ C1/C3 nitrosamine p=6.8×10⁻¹⁰, Mixed L. reuteri probiotic), and a 185-participant independent KMUH cohort shows **Streptococcus anginosus (SA) prevalence in gastric juice increases from 47.3% (no IM) → 56.9% (IM) → 86.4% (cancer)** with inverse association to tight-junction proteins (CLDN18.2/OCLN/ZO-1). Multilayer proteome–immune integration defines **3 NAT initiation ecologies** (HP-driven inflammatory PN3, non-HP microbiome-enriched immune-silent PN2, HP-free depleted PN1) and **4 tumour proteome clusters PT1–PT4** plus **HP-negative subtypes HP_N T1–T3** with **SA-linked barrier-loss T2** (EMT/IL-17/NOD-like/NF-κB/PI3K-AKT) and **CLDN18.2-high epithelial-stable T1** (42% met 43-14A IHC criteria, late-stage diffuse Bormann type 4). Survival decision-tree integrates stage + DBAC + proteome–immune state and identifies **stage II-III + PT1+IM2/IM3 as exceeding stage IV mortality**. Kinase enrichment 3 (KEA3) maps anatomy-specific upstream kinases (Loc 1 cardia TP53/MAPK; Loc 4 antrum MET/MYC) and nominates **CDK4 as a shared actionable hub** across IHC-positive, proteome-defined-positive (HER2/PD-L1/CLDN18.2 high), and IHC-negative subgroups (49.2% IHC-negative). 5 prognostic biomarkers in Location 4 antrum (SHROOM1 / p-LYST S2627 / XYLT1 / CRIP1 / SPATS2L) validated in KM Plotter (n=118–875).

## Key Points

### Cohort + multi-omic coverage — 154 treatment-naïve Taiwanese, 4-layer molecular + microbiome + IHC

- 154 patients, NTUH + KMUH 2022-03 to 2024-12, median 67 yr, 62% male, all East Asian Han Chinese; 25% stage I; predominantly antral; East-Asia intestinal:diffuse ratio. AJCC 8th staging.
- WES 14,134 nonsynonymous mutated genes; RNA-seq 19,504 transcripts; TMT proteome >10,000 protein groups; phosphoproteome 30,000 phosphosites; IHC for MMR / HER2 / CLDN18.2 / PD-L1; microbial RNA from unmapped RNA-seq reads aligned to HRGMv2 (MAPQ>10, prevalence ≥5%) + microbial protein via UniProt (FDR<0.01, ≥5 unique proteins/species, ≥10% prevalence).
- HP status: dual IgG ELISA + tissue pathology — HP_E (exposed) 69.5%, HP_N (never exposed) 30.5%, HP_A (active) 22.7% confirmed by culture + CagA PCR (CF2/CR2 primers).
- TP53 + ARID1A mutual exclusivity (p=0.002); KMT2D 24% vs TCGA 9–16% (3-fold higher); KMT2C enrichment; CDH1 mutation enriched in females (p<0.001), diffuse (p=0.013), female diffuse (p=0.008); PIK3CA 11% (lower than TCGA 12–26% Caucasian).
- Independent validation cohort: 185 KMUH endoscopy participants (saliva n=159, IM plaque n=151, gastric juice n=101, stool n=93).

### Environmental carcinogen signature — DBAC PAH is East-Asia–specific, prognostically dominant

- NMF deconvolution of tumour + NAT mutation spectra against COSMIC + experimentally derived carcinogen DBs identifies **7 signatures G1–G7**: G1 spontaneous 5-mC deamination, G2 tumour MMR, G3 polymerase-η translesion, G4 NAT MMR, G5 DSB repair, G6 nitrosamine, G7 irradiation; plus **DBAC** (dibenz[a,h]acridine, PAH class) and nitrosamine-like patterns.
- Unsupervised clustering on signature contributions → **6 carcinogen clusters C1–C6**:
  - C1 (MMR-related NAT), C2 (persistent deamination), C3 (nitrosamine-like NAT — C3 NAT shows CYP2E1 ↑ + organonitrogen metabolism, supporting nitrosamine exposure origin).
  - C4 (DBAC tumour-only), C5 (DBAC tumour+NAT), C6 (DBAC NAT-dominant).
- DBAC tumour clusters (C4-C5): EFS p=0.02, multivariable adjusted **HR 2.36–3.16** (age/sex/stage adjusted); diffuse subtype DBAC presence confers **5.9× progression risk** (p=0.013); regulatory T-cell fraction p=0.002 immunosuppressive "cold" TME.
- 3 carcinogen exposure class cell-line tests in MKN28 (intestinal) + MKN45 (diffuse): PAH (benzo[a]pyrene BaP, DBAC-related) / nitro-PAH (1-nitropyrene 1-NP) / nitrosamines (NDMA, NDBA, NDELA, NPYR). Boyden chamber 48 h IC10 / 1/5 IC10: **BaP MKN45 3.7× invasion, MKN28 2.7×** (p<0.05 vs untreated); nitrosamines modest or non-significant. PAH-treated cells show concordance with DBAC tissue signature pathways (cell migration / matrix adhesion / neutrophil degranulation / defence regulation / CD40 signalling / p53).
- Cross-cohort DBAC signature comparison: detected in Taiwanese + Japanese gastric cancer cohorts but **absent in TCGA** (Western-dominated) — region-specific dietary PAH (charcoal-grilling, pan-frying; cooked BaP 1.30 ng/g vs raw 0.12 ng/g).
- Pathway activation in DBAC clusters: PI3K-AKT + ERBB + VEGF + CD40 (NFAT-mediated migration/invasion convergent hub); Laurén-stratified — diffuse cytoskeletal/adhesion (actin/paxillin/Robo-Slit), intestinal RhoGDI/invadopodia.

### Microbiome — 4 RNA + protein clusters, Streptococcus anginosus as HP-independent driver

- Microbial RNA + protein cross-validated 14 species (≥5 unique microbial proteins per species, FDR<0.01); patient-level Spearman |ρ|≥0.40 networks: N-N (NAT-NAT) denser than T-T (mean degree 1.31 vs 0.50, clustering 0.315 vs 0.233, lower modularity 0.593 vs 0.872) — integrated mucosal ecology. NAT hubs: Halalkalibacterium halodurans, Novosphingobium aromaticivorans, Staphylococcus epidermidis, Acetivibrio thermocellus, Halothermothrix orenii, Syntrophus aciditrophicus, HP.
- 4 MB.RNA clusters with linked transcriptome G1–G4 patterns:
  - **Microvirga-dominant**: DBAC link p=9.3×10⁻¹², co-occurs with M. pneumoniae (gastric carcinogenesis implicated); G2 enrichment peroxisome / xenobiotic metabolism / CYP P450.
  - **HP-dominant**: HP_E patient enrichment p=4.97×10⁻⁸, IM1 + PN3, antibacterial immune G3–G4.
  - **Streptococcus-dominant**: contains Streptococcus species + co-occurring M. pneumoniae + V. cholerae; younger age (p=0.018); C1+C3 link (p=6.8×10⁻¹⁰); G1 cytoskeletal/apical-junction/hedgehog + reduced DNA repair; early-onset trajectory.
  - **Mixed**: Limosilactobacillus reuteri (probiotic) enriched; immune-cold Immune N2/N4 (p=0.005); tolerant gastric microenvironment.
- Bivariate co-occurrence (φ coefficient) between microbial presence + host mutational signatures:
  - **DBAC ↔ Microvirga φ=0.50 (p<0.001), Cardiobacterium φ=0.53 (p<0.001), F. nucleatum φ=0.20 (p=0.01)** — PAH-rich consortium.
  - Nitrosamine G6 ↔ Desulfovibrio φ=0.31 (p<0.001), no abundance correlation — "permissive threshold" model (bacterial presence alone fuels nitrosamine production via nitrate-reducing capacity).

### NAT proteome–immune integration — 3 initiation ecologies, sex-subtype-location axis

- NAT 3 proteome clusters (PN1–PN3) + tumour 4 clusters (PT1–PT4) + NAT 4 immune clusters (IM1–IM4) + tumour 5 (IM1–IM5).
- Cross-layer alignment in NAT — Sankey: **PN3 = canonical HP-driven**, intestinal type, IM1 immune-active, CagA+; **PN1 = female/diffuse**, mixed HP, partial immune activation; **PN2 = non-HP microbiome-enriched immune-silent antral** (IM2 overlap, F. nucleatum + N. aromaticivorans xenobiotic estrogen activator). MCA1 (HP+ intestinal), MCA2 (PN2/IM2 F. nucleatum + N. aromaticivorans), MCA3 (female diffuse mixed HP, lacking dominant microbe).
- Pathway: PN3 immune/inflammatory (CagA virulence, adhesion/ECM); PN1 adhesion/ECM limited immune; PN2 cell-cycle / DNA replication / oxidative metabolism — proliferation-driven low-inflammation; no pathogen-sensing.
- **Tumour PT1-IM bifurcation** (p=0.025): PT1-IM1 (Fcγ receptor phagocytosis + neutrophil extracellular trap) excellent prognosis; PT1-IM3 (T/B-cell activation without supportive immune programmes) **worst OS, exceeds stage IV mortality** in decision tree. PT2 matrix/cytoskeleton without immune; PT3 metabolic + OXPHOS; PT4 DNA repair + protein translation. PT1↔Bormann type 3 (p=0.002), PT2↔HP+ (p=0.003), PT3↔diffuse (p=0.02), PT4↔intestinal (p=0.01).
- Higher microbial richness in NAT > tumour — commensal depletion during tumourigenesis.

### HP-negative subtypes — SA-linked barrier-loss T2 + CLDN18.2-high epithelial-stable T1

- Among HP_N tumours (n=47): 3 proteome clusters T1–T3:
  - **T2**: bacterial invasion (Streptococcus anginosus enrichment) + immune activation (S. aureus / IL-17 / NOD-like receptor signalling). Significant **CLDN18.2/OCLN/ZO-1 reduction** (Wilcoxon adj. p<0.05) with concordant FN1/THBS1/PDGFRB ↑ + PI3K-AKT — EMT-biased, protumourigenic.
  - **T1**: barrier-intact CLDN18.2-retained; reduced innate immune (C3/C5/MBL2), NF-κB (RELA/TRAF3/CXCL3), NOD-like (RIPK2/PYCARD), IL-17 (PTGS2), EMT (FN1/THBS1) — epithelial, immune-quiescent state. Enriched aggressive phenotypes: late stage, diffuse, Bormann type 4. **42% of T1 tumours met CLDN18.2-targeted therapy IHC criteria (43-14A antibody)**.
  - **T3**: post-transcriptional regulation + RNA processing.
- HP_N NAT subtypes N1–N3: N1 nitrosamine-like (C3, p<0.05) + OXPHOS / thermogenic remodelling; N2 immune-silent (IM4, p<0.0001) + chemical/drug metabolism; N3 modest immune activation.
- 185-participant independent KMUH cohort: **SA detection in gastric juice progressively increased — no IM 47.3% → IM 56.9% → gastric cancer 86.4%** (saliva / plaque / stool show no such pattern). ELISA: SA-positive ↔ CLDN18 / OCLN / ZO-1 ↓ (Student's t, p<0.05). Gastric-juice SA inversely correlates with junction proteins in selected proteogenomic cases.

### Tumour location proteogenomics — 4 anatomy clusters refine binary classification

- 4 anatomy clusters (Loc 1 upper body/cardia, Loc 2-3 mid-lower body, Loc 4 antrum) vs binary cardia/non-cardia: **mean η² 0.257 vs 0.077**, paired Wilcoxon p=0.00386, median η² gain 0.108 (95% CI 0.033–0.455) across 14 anatomic axes.
- Tumour location-specific pathways: Loc 1 TP53/MAPK/DNA-damage/motility/cytokines; Loc 2 cell cycle/DNA repair/WNT; Loc 3 EMT/EGFR/CDH1/TNF (aggressive — higher M stage, larger tumours); Loc 4 MET/MYC/inflammatory cytokine (concordant with antral MET enrichment).
- KEA3 upstream kinases (FDA-approved drugs): broad MAPK3+MTOR+SRC; location-specific — **Loc 2: ABL1/MAPK11/PRKCA/PRKCG/RET/ROCK1/ROCK2/SYK**; **Loc 3: BRAF/CDK4/ERBB2/FGFR3/RAF1**; **Loc 4: MET/PRKAA2**.
- Laurén × location: diffuse Loc 2-3 adhesion/ECM/FCGR (NF-κB/EMT/FCGR); diffuse Loc 1 mTOR/neutrophil degranulation/ribosome; diffuse Loc 4 FCGR/lipid metabolism; intestinal Loc 1 EMT/NF-κB/p53/VEGF; intestinal Loc 2 DNA repair/NOTCH/proteasome; intestinal Loc 3 FGFR/innate immune; intestinal Loc 4 adhesion/N-glycan/cell cycle.
- Sex–hormone–location: intestinal-type (male-predominant) Loc 1+4 p53/androgen; diffuse-type (female-enriched) Loc 3 oestrogen-response.
- Cancer hallmark druggable mapping: Loc 1 HDAC1+EZH2 (TP53/DNA-damage chromatin remodelling–DNA repair axis); Loc 4 MET activation.
- 5 prognostic biomarkers in Loc 4 antrum (Cox HR>1, p<0.05): **SHROOM1 / p-LYST (S2627) / XYLT1 / CRIP1 / SPATS2L** — KM Plotter validation (SHROOM1 n=631, LYST n=875, XYLT1 n=631, CRIP1 n=118, SPATS2L n=875).

### Decision-tree prognostic model + CDK4 as actionable hub

- EFS decision tree: stage I-II (good) → stage III-IV with high tumour DBAC contribution = **worst EFS, exceeds stage IV no DBAC**; stage III-IV no DBAC = stage IV worst.
- OS decision tree: stage IV worst; among stage I-III, **C3 (nitrosamine NAT) clusters show superior OS**; **stage II-III + PT1+IM2/IM3 exceeds stage IV OS mortality**.
- IHC-stratified druggable phosphoproteome:
  - **IHC+ Biomarker group** (MMR / HER2 / CLDN18.2 / PD-L1+): 50% (n=68) — JAK3 / CDK4 / IKBKB / PKC.
  - **Non-IHC proteome group** (HER2 / PD-L1 / CLDN18.2-high by proteome only): 26% (n=34) — ERBB2 / CDK4.
  - **Non-IHC other** (no markers): rest — CDK4 / EGFR / IKBKB / PKC.
- CDK4 common hub; tumour CDK4 phosphorylation ↑ + downstream substrates FOXM1 / RB1 / RBL2 / WDR77 phosphorylation ↑ confirmed (Wilcoxon ** / ***).
- 49.2% of total cohort IHC-negative for all standard markers → proteome/phosphoproteome-guided combination strategies.

### Limitations + clinical caveats explicit in paper

- DBAC signature region-specific (East Asia dietary); generalisation requires multi-ethnic cohort validation.
- CDK4/6 inhibitor single-agent in upper GI cancers shows minimal activity (palbociclib phase II median PFS 1.8 mo, OS 3.0 mo, ref 117); paper explicitly cautions against unselected CDK4/6 use — combination therapy needed (e.g., MEK + CDK4/6 + autophagy blockade in KRAS-mutated, Zhou et al. ref 118).
- Patient and public not involved in study design.
- Single-region cohort (NTUH + KMUH only); cross-population transferability requires external multi-ethnic replication.
- DBAC-microbiome co-occurrence is observational; mechanistic causal link between Microvirga/Cardiobacterium/F. nucleatum and PAH bioactivation requires direct testing.
- HP_N T2 SA-linked barrier loss in proteogenomic cases; ELISA validation in 185-participant cohort but no in vivo mechanistic SA model.

## Methods

**Cohort**: NTUH + KMUH 2022-03 to 2024-12, n=154 treatment-naïve gastric cancer, ≥20 yr, median 67 (range 37–89), 62% male, all East Asian Han Chinese. IRB AS-IRB-BM-17056 (Academia Sinica), 202110085RINB (NTUH), KMUHIRB-F(I)-20210214 / E(II)-20230170 / F(I)-20240523 (KMUH). Written informed consent per Declaration of Helsinki.

**Independent validation cohort**: 185 KMUH endoscopy participants (saliva n=159, IM plaque n=151, gastric juice n=101, stool n=93).

**Samples**: Paired tumour + adjacent normal mucosa (NAT) + peripheral blood mononuclear cell (PBMC).

**Molecular profiling**:
- WES: 14,134 nonsynonymous mutation genes (Table S1C).
- RNA-seq: 19,504 transcripts (Table S1D); unmapped reads → HRGMv2 (Human Reference Gut Microbiome v2) for microbial RNA, MAPQ>10, ≥5% prevalence (Table S3A-C).
- TMT proteome: >10,000 protein groups (Tables S1E, F).
- Ti⁴⁺-IMAC phosphoproteome: 30,000 phosphosites (Tables S1G, H).
- Microbial protein: UniProt microbial + viral reference, FDR<0.01, ≥5 unique proteins/species, ≥10% prevalence (Tables S3D, E).
- IHC: MMR / HER2 / CLDN18.2 (43-14A antibody) / PD-L1.

**HP detection**: Dual IgG ELISA + tissue pathology → HP_E / HP_N status. Active infection (HP_A) confirmed by Brucella chocolate agar microaerobic culture (5% O₂ / 10% CO₂ / 85% N₂, 7 days) + CagA PCR (CF2 5'-GAATCAGTATTTTTCAGAC-3' / CR2 5'-GGGTTGTATGATATTTTCC-3' primers, 95°C 10 min + 35 cycles 95/55/72°C 30 s + 72°C 10 min elongation).

**Mutational signatures**: NMF on tumour + NAT mutation spectra, COSMIC + experimentally-derived carcinogen DB (Kucab et al. Cell 2019) cosine similarity reference. 7 signatures (G1–G7). DBAC + nitrosamine-like additional patterns. 6 carcinogen clusters (C1–C6) by patient signature contributions.

**Cell line invasion**: MKN28 (intestinal) + MKN45 (diffuse) RPMI-1640 / 10% FBS / penicillin-streptomycin. 48-h Boyden chamber after IC10 / 1/5 IC10 exposure to BaP (PAH), 1-NP (nitro-PAH), NDMA / NDBA / NDELA / NPYR (nitrosamines). MTT for cytotoxicity.

**Clustering**: NMF (multi-omic integration), unsupervised hierarchical clustering (microbiome / proteome), MCA (multiple correspondence analysis for proteome-immune-microbe-clinical).

**Pathway / kinase enrichment**: MSigDB (Fisher's exact, adj. p<0.05), IPA (activating z-score>1), KEGG (STRING v12, FDR<0.05), Cytoscape v3.10.3 PPI (4,007 nodes / 92,001 edges), KEA3 upstream kinase + FDA-approved drugs (Fisher's exact, adj. p<0.05).

**Survival**: Cox multivariable (age + sex + stage), Kaplan-Meier log-rank, decision-tree (interpretable). External KM Plotter for biomarker validation (n=118–875).

**Data availability** (controlled-access):
- WES + RNA-seq: GDC + dbGaP **phs004447.v1.p1**.
- Proteomic + phosphoproteomic: PDC **PDC000645** (proteome) + **PDC000649** (phospho).
- Data are de-identified, subject to repository data-use policies.

**Funding**: AS-KPQ-107-TCPMP + AS-KPQ-111-KNT (Academia Sinica Next-Generation Pathway of Taiwan Cancer Precision Medicine Program) + MOHW 114-TDU-B-221-144008 + NSTC 114-2740-B-002-002 + KMU-TB114002. Collaboration with NCI ICPC + CPTAC.

**Competing interests**: None declared.

## Cancer Multiomics Project Relevance

1. **East Asia gastric cancer reference cohort (n=154, Han Chinese) — 한국인 GC 코호트 설계 시 직접 비교 + ICPC/CPTAC 통합 노드**. NTUH+KMUH treatment-naïve / matched T+NAT / 4-layer proteogenomics + 30,000 phosphosites는 한미암 GC cohort에 동일 protocol 적용 가능. TP53 + ARID1A mutual exclusion (p=0.002) + KMT2D 24% Asian-specific + CDH1 in female diffuse (p=0.008)은 한국 GC cohort에서 1차 cross-validation target. Mun 2019 EOGC + Jiao 2025 pangenome + Wang 2025 spatial-TLS gastric paper와 함께 East Asia gastric proteogenomic ladder 구성 (Section 1). dbGaP phs004447 + PDC000645/649 raw data 접근으로 한미암 cohort cross-reference / 재분석 가능.

2. **DBAC PAH 환경 노출 시그니처 — 한국인 GC 식이 PAH 노출 (charcoal-grilling, pan-frying) 1차 검증 대상**. DBAC signature이 Taiwanese + Japanese에는 존재하지만 TCGA(Western)에서는 검출 안 됨 — 한국인 코호트에서 동일 DBAC signature 존재 확인 + EFS HR (Taiwan HR 2.36–3.16, diffuse 5.9× p=0.013) 재현성 검증이 가장 즉시 가능한 East Asian comparative analysis. BaP 식이 노출 (cooked food 1.30 ng/g vs raw 0.12 ng/g)은 한국 grilled meat 소비 패턴에 직접 매핑 가능. 한미암 환자 식이 questionnaire + DBAC mutational signature 결합으로 environmental-exposure-informed risk stratification 설계 근거.

3. **HP-negative gastric cancer SA-linked barrier-loss 가설 + CLDN18.2-high epithelial-stable subtype** — 한미암 HP-negative GC 환자 stratification 시 (1) gastric juice SA 측정 + (2) CLDN18.2/OCLN/ZO-1 IHC 동시 측정 protocol 채택의 직접 근거. Taiwan SA gastric juice 분포 (no IM 47.3% → IM 56.9% → cancer 86.4%)는 한국 cohort 재현성 검증 1차 target. 42% T1 CLDN18.2-targeted therapy 적합성 (43-14A IHC criteria)은 한미암 HP-negative 환자 zolbetuximab/Claudiximab 적합성 평가 logic. SA가 단순 marker 아닌 mechanism (EMT/IL-17/NOD-like/NF-κB/PI3K-AKT) 통해 barrier 파괴 → 한미암 anti-inflammatory / anti-fibrotic / PI3K-directed therapy 후보군 평가 axis.

4. **PT1-IM 분기 + 4-anatomy location proteogenomics — 한미암 GC stratification에서 단순 stage 너머 multilayer integration 모델 직접 차용**. PT1-IM1 (Fcγ phago + NET) vs PT1-IM3 (T/B-cell) bifurcation (p=0.025)이 stage IV mortality를 능가하는 분기점이라는 사실은 한미암 GC cohort에서 immune cluster sub-stratification이 stage 단독보다 강한 prognostic 가치를 가질 수 있음을 시사. 4-cluster anatomy refinement (η² 0.257 vs binary 0.077)은 단순 cardia/non-cardia 분류 한계를 넘어 한미암 코호트의 14-axis anatomical mapping 도입 근거. KEA3 location-specific upstream kinase mapping (Loc 2 ABL1/SYK/ROCK, Loc 3 BRAF/CDK4/ERBB2, Loc 4 MET/PRKAA2)으로 한미암 GC tumour 위치별 kinase inhibitor library 우선순위 차별화 가능.

5. **CDK4 actionable hub + decision-tree 모델 — 한미암 GC IHC-negative 환자군 (49.2%) targeted therapy 확장 logic**. IHC+ / proteome-defined HER2/PD-L1/CLDN18.2 high / non-IHC other 3-tier stratification 모두에서 CDK4 phospho + 다운스트림 FOXM1/RB1/RBL2/WDR77 ↑ — 한미암 GC 코호트에서 phosphoproteome-guided CDK4/6 combination 임상시험 설계 직접 reference. 단, 본문 명시: CDK4/6 단독제 phase II palbociclib 상지 GI cancer 효과 미미 (median PFS 1.8 mo, OS 3.0 mo) → 비선택적 CDK4/6 사용 금지, MEK + CDK4/6 + autophagy combo 등 selected combination 필수. Decision-tree (EFS: stage + DBAC; OS: stage + C3 + PT1+IM2/3)는 한미암 GC interpretable prognostic model 베이스라인.
