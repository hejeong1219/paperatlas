## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

This section was added for the interactive atlas on recent multi-omics proteomics/PTM identification scale. Values are taken from the local PDF and preserve the paper's own reporting unit.

- Cohort/scope: 140 pancreatic cancer cases; 67 NATs and 8 normal ductal tissues in MS workflow.
- Proteome: 11,662 (identified and quantified proteins).
- Phosphoproteome: 51,469 (identified and quantified phosphosites).
- Acetylome: not assayed or not reported as a single extractable count (not assayed/reported).
- MS method: TMT-11; bRPLC; Fe-IMAC phosphopeptide enrichment; glycopeptide enrichment.
- Instrument/platform: Easy nLC 1200 UHPLC coupled to Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.
- Extraction evidence: Results text: proteomics, phosphoproteomics, and glycoproteomics identified and quantified 11,662 proteins, 51,469 phosphosites, and 34,024 glycopeptides.
- Interpretation note: This study used glycoproteomics rather than acetylomics.
