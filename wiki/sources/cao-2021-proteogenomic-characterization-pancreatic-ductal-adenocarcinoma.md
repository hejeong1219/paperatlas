# Proteogenomic characterization of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma

_Cell, 2021._

## Summary

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) is a highly aggressive cancer with poor survival. This paper reports an integrated proteogenomic analysis of **140 PDACs**, **67 normal-adjacent tissues**, and **9 normal pancreatic ductal tissues**, combining proteome/phosphoproteome/glycoproteome profiling with WGS/WES, methylation, RNA-seq, and miRNA-seq to connect genomic alterations to protein abundance, signaling pathways, and PTMs.

## Key Points

- Cohort-scale PDAC **proteome + phosphoproteome + glycoproteome** plus matched WGS/WES and transcriptome/epigenome layers enables genome-to-functional-state interpretation beyond RNA alone.
- Explicit multi-strategy **tumor cellularity (neoplastic content) assessment** is treated as a prerequisite for robust downstream multi-omics modeling.
- Designed as a community resource for PDAC biology, early detection hypothesis generation, and target nomination from proteogenomic and PTM signaling evidence.


## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

This section was added for the interactive atlas on recent multi-omics proteomics/PTM identification scale. Values are taken from the local PDF and preserve the paper's own reporting unit.

- Cohort/scope: 140 pancreatic cancer cases; 67 NATs and 8 normal ductal tissues in MS workflow.
- Proteome: 11,662 (identified and quantified proteins).
- Phosphoproteome: 51,469 (identified and quantified phosphosites).
- Acetylome: not assayed or not reported as a single extractable count (not assayed/reported).
- MS method: TMT-11; bRPLC; Fe-IMAC phosphopeptide enrichment; glycopeptide enrichment.
- Instrument/platform: Easy nLC 1200 UHPLC coupled to Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.
- Extraction evidence: Results text: proteomics, phosphoproteomics, and glycoproteomics identified and quantified 11,662 proteins, 51,469 phosphosites, and 34,024 glycopeptides.
- Interpretation note: This study used glycoproteomics rather than acetylomics.

## Cancer Multiomics Project Relevance

- Cancer Multiomics 과제의 핵심인 **WGS–(phospho)proteome 통합** 관점에서, PDAC처럼 유전적 이벤트(KRAS/TP53 등)와 기능 상태(kinase signaling/PTM)가 반드시 1:1로 대응하지 않는 상황을 “측정 기반”으로 해석하는 레퍼런스다.
- cohort 단위에서 **tumor purity/세포성 보정의 중요성**을 강조하고 있어, WGS 변이/복제수 신호와 phosphoproteome 차이를 결합해 반응성/내성 feature를 설계할 때 전처리 체크리스트로 재사용 가능하다.
