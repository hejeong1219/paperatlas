# LSM14B is an Oocyte-Specific RNA-Binding Protein Indispensable for Maternal mRNA Metabolism and Oocyte Development in Mice

_Advanced Science, 2023._

## Summary

Mammalian oogenesis depends on maternal mRNAs that are produced and stored during the oocyte growth phase and later used while the oocyte is transcriptionally silent. RNA-binding proteins (RBPs) govern the storage, translation, and decay of these maternal messages, yet the full RBP complement of oocytes was unknown. This study uses mRNA interactome capture (RIC) to define the mouse oocyte RBPome (1396 putative RBPs) and selects LSM family member 14B (LSM14B) for in-depth analysis. LSM14B is shown to be oocyte-specific (whereas its paralog LSM14A is somatic-restricted), and Lsm14b knockout causes female-specific infertility with oocytes that fail to complete meiosis I-to-II progression and arrest at an interphase/pronucleus-like state. Using LACE-Seq, IP/MS, polysome profiling, transcriptomics, and proteomics, the authors show LSM14B binds thousands of maternal mRNAs and hundreds of proteins, selectively promotes translation of a subset of oogenesis-related transcripts (e.g., Mastl), colocalizes with DDX6 in P-body-like puncta that form via liquid-liquid phase separation, and acts as an RNP interaction hub coordinating maternal mRNA fate and oocyte development.

## Key Points

- mRNA interactome capture (RIC) on 50,000 fully-grown mouse oocytes (two replicates: 1149 and 549 RBPs) identified 1396 total putative oocyte RBPs; 632 overlapped the EMBL RBPbase ("confirmed RBPs"), 764 were "unknown RBPs," and a stringent set (MS-peptide ≥2) gave a 270-protein RBPome (228 in RBPbase). Top RBDs were RRM, DEAD, Helicase-C, ZnF-C2H2, and KH.
- LSM14B is oocyte-specific, expressed in oocytes at all follicle stages but absent from granulosa cells and other tissues; the paralog LSM14A is broadly somatic (cumulus/mural granulosa cells, many tissues) and barely detectable in fully-grown oocytes, suggesting LSM14A/LSM14B functional divergence in vertebrates.
- Lsm14b-KO (Lsm14b^tm1a(KOMP)Mbp null allele) females are infertile while KO males are fertile; KO females ovulate slightly fewer eggs, and KO eggs fail in vitro fertilization (no cleavage or blastocyst, gradual degeneration).
- ~80% of Lsm14b-KO ovulated eggs arrest at interphase with a pronucleus (PN)-like structure instead of arresting at metaphase II; MI-to-anaphase I transition and first polar body emission are delayed 3-5 h.
- 73.03% of KO MI-oocytes show prolonged BUBR1 (BUB1B) kinetochore staining (extended spindle assembly checkpoint); the MPS1/TTK inhibitor AZ3146 fully rescues prolonged MI and delayed PBE, indicating the defect is failure to initiate anaphase (delayed APC activation) rather than checkpoint-triggering errors.
- Transcriptomics found 2285 DEGs (970 up, 1315 down) in KO GV-oocytes; meiotic regulators were misexpressed (up: Bub1, Bub3, Ccnb2, Esco2, Fbxo5/Emi1, Mapk3, Plk1, Tpx2, Wee1, Wee2; down: Anapc10, Cdc26, Cks2). Elevated WEE1/pCDK1-Y15 explains the failure to reach MII; the WEE inhibitor PD166285 induces MII in KO oocytes.
- Proteomics found 326 differentially abundant proteins; >84% had no matching mRNA change (post-transcriptional control). MASTL protein was nearly absent in KO oocytes despite unchanged Mastl mRNA, and Mastl-mRNA microinjection raised MII numbers ~50%, implicating loss of MASTL translation in the meiotic defect.
- LACE-Seq mapped 78,741 binding peaks across 9253 LSM14B-bound mRNAs (15% of sites in 3'UTRs; CCUCUC the most enriched hexamer), including oogenesis "star factors" (PATL2, YBX2, ZAR1, CKS2, MOS, WEE2, GDF9, GPR3, PDE3A, DPPA3, NPM2, TRIM28, NLRP5, PADI6, TLE6, BRWD1, FOXO3, NOBOX). A 3'UTR mKate reporter assay confirmed LSM14B binding to the Mastl 3'UTR promotes its translation.
- Polysome-Seq showed 1465 transcripts with altered translational efficiency (TE) in KO oocytes; 984 (66.3%) are LSM14B-bound, of which 733 (74.5%) had lower TE without KO — i.e., LSM14B predominantly promotes (not represses) translation of a select mRNA class.
- IP/MS (~8000 GV-oocytes) found 305 LSM14B protein partners enriched in RNA metabolism, oocyte SCMC formation, and gamete generation, including DDX6, EIF4E1B, EIF4ENIF1 (4E-T), MARF1, PATL2, YBX2, ZAR1, NLRP5, PADI6, TLE6, and methylation regulators (IGF2BP2, YTHDF2, DNMT1, UHRF1); DDX6, PATL2, YBX2, EIF4ENIF1, and SPIN1 were downregulated in KO oocytes.
- LSM14B colocalizes with DDX6 in P-body-like granular foci in non-growing primordial-follicle-stage oocytes and in HEK293 cells; 21 LSM14B IP partners overlap the Hubstenberger P-body proteome, supporting LSM14B as an oocyte P-body component. FRAP of LSM14B-mKate puncta (recovery within ~10 s, near-max by ~50 s) indicates formation via liquid-liquid phase separation.
- LSM14B binding to MARF1 (an oocyte-specific ribonuclease) but not DCP1/DCP2 suggests decapping-independent maternal mRNA turnover; LSM14B's P-body localization in early oocytes is consistent with its later MARDO (mitochondria-associated ribonucleoprotein domain) localization reported by Cheng et al.

## Methods

- **mRNA interactome capture (RIC):** UV crosslinking (254 nm) plus oligo(dT)25 magnetic-bead capture on 50,000 GV-stage fully-grown oocytes (2 replicates of 25,000), RNase elution, LC-MS/MS (Orbitrap Velos), MaxQuant search vs mouse UniProt. RBPome dataset PXD034071.
- **Genetics/phenotyping:** Lsm14b^tm1a(KOMP)Mbp knockout-first allele on C57BL/6J; PCR genotyping (652 bp WT, 270 bp KO); 8-month fertility test (4 WT, 6 KO females); superovulation egg counts; in vitro maturation, live-cell imaging (GFP-tubulin, mCherry-histone, Venus-CCNB1, mCherry-PTTG1), IVF and embryo culture to blastocyst.
- **Pharmacology:** AZ3146 (MPS1/TTK inhibitor) and PD166285 (WEE kinase inhibitor) used to probe SAC and CDK1 regulation.
- **Omics:** RNA-Seq (GSE204773, cuffdiff, FDR <0.05), microproteomics on low-input oocytes (PXD034070, Welch's t-test, fold >1.2 & p <0.05), LACE-Seq for single-nucleotide-resolution RNA binding (GSE206190), polysome profiling/Polysome-Seq for translational efficiency (GSE206270), Co-IP/IP-MS for protein partners (PXD034185).
- **Functional assays:** Mastl-mRNA microinjection rescue; mKate-Mastl-3'UTR reporter (complete vs LSM14B-binding-site-deleted, binding site TAGTGATGGAGTCTCACTGC) with EGFP control; immunofluorescence colocalization of LSM14B/DDX6; FRAP of LSM14B-mKate condensates in early-growing oocytes and HEK293 cells.

## Open Questions

- Does Lsm14b deletion disrupt the integrity and function of the oocyte MARDO (mitochondria-associated ribonucleoprotein domain), where LSM14B localizes during later oocyte stages?
- What determines whether LSM14B promotes translation versus repression/turnover of a given bound mRNA (the apparent context-dependence of its action)?
- Through what decapping-independent mechanism (e.g., via MARF1) does LSM14B contribute to maternal mRNA turnover?
- Do human LSM14B mutations contribute to oocyte maturation defects, premature ovarian insufficiency, or infertility, given the maternal-effect phenotypes seen in mice?
- What accounts for the slight reduction in ovulated egg number — a subtle perturbation of follicular development not resolved here?
