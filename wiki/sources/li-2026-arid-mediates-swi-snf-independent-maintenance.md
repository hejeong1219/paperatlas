# ARID1A Mediates SWI/SNF-Independent Maintenance of Heterochromatin Architecture to Restrain Viral Mimicry and Immunogenicity in Colon Cancer

_Cancer Research, 2026._

## Summary

ARID1A (BAF250a) is a signature subunit of the SWI/SNF chromatin-remodeling complex and one of the most frequently mutated tumor suppressors in human cancer, whose activity has been ascribed almost entirely to SWI/SNF-dependent nucleosome remodeling. This study establishes a **SWI/SNF-independent** function of ARID1A: it physically tethers the heterochromatin scaffold **TRIM28** to preserve H3K9me3-marked heterochromatin over repetitive elements, thereby silencing **viral mimicry** and restraining tumor immunogenicity in colorectal cancer. Endogenous ARID1A immunoprecipitation–mass spectrometry from HCT116 cells and colon cancer patient tissue identified TRIM28 as a high-confidence partner; the ARID1A–TRIM28 interaction (TRIM28 aa 376–625) persisted after knockdown of the SWI/SNF ATPases SMARCA2 or SMARCA4. ARID1A ChIP-seq found 29,641 peaks, 70% (20,850) at repeat elements (α-satellite SATα and HERV/ERV LTRs), coinciding with H3K9me3. Loss of ARID1A did not change bulk levels of TRIM28, SETDB1, or HP1α but **selectively dislodged the H3K9 methyltransferase SETDB1 from TRIM28**, eroding H3K9me3 and SETDB1 from repeat loci while TRIM28 remained chromatin-bound but methyltransferase-deficient. This derepressed satellites, ERVs, and type-I interferon genes (ISGs) concordantly across MSI (HCT116, DLD-1) and MSS (A375, HeLa) lines, irrespective of microsatellite status. The phenotype was epistatic to TRIM28 (TRIM28 knockdown phenocopied and was not augmented by ARID1A loss; TRIM28Δ376–624 failed to rescue) but uncoupled from SWI/SNF: the SWI/SNF ATPase inhibitor BD98 and SMARCA2/SMARCA4/SMARCAD1 loss induced ISG15/CXCL10 but not satellite/ERV transcription. A cell-penetrating **TAT-peptide** spanning TRIM28 aa 376–625 competitively displaced ARID1A from TRIM28, provoked viral mimicry in ARID1A-proficient tumors, and—delivered intratumorally—converted immunologically "cold" CT26 tumors into CD8+ T cell–inflamed lesions and suppressed tumor growth. The IFN response and the heightened sensitivity of ARID1A-deficient tumors to anti–PD-1 required both cytosolic DNA (cGAS/STING) and RNA (RIG-I/DDX58, MDA5) sensors; reverse-transcriptase inhibitor lamivudine (3TC) and major-satellite LNAs blunted ISG induction and tumor growth. The work nominates the ARID1A–TRIM28 interface as a druggable immune rheostat for potentiating checkpoint therapy.

## Key Points

- **ARID1A binds TRIM28 independently of SWI/SNF.** Endogenous ARID1A IP–MS from HCT116 cells and a colon cancer patient sample identified TRIM28 (12 peptides) as a high-confidence partner; the interaction mapped to TRIM28 aa 376–625 and persisted after SMARCA2 or SMARCA4 knockdown, establishing complex formation independent of SWI/SNF ATPase activity (Fig. 1A–G).
- **ARID1A occupies repeat-element heterochromatin coincident with H3K9me3.** ChIP-seq found 29,641 ARID1A peaks, 70% (20,850) at repeat elements, mainly α-satellite (SATα) and HERV/ERV LTRs; ARID1A binding overlapped H3K9me3 at 31.7% of repeat elements vs 2.3% genome-wide, with no enrichment for active marks H3K4me3 or H3K27ac (Fig. 2C–D).
- **ARID1A loss triggers viral mimicry irrespective of MSI status.** ARID1A depletion reduced heterochromatic foci in MSI-high HCT116, made repeats gain active marks (H3K4me3, H4K9ac, H4ac) and lose repressive marks, and concordantly upregulated satellite repeats, ERVs, and type-I IFN genes across MSI (HCT116, DLD-1) and MSS (A375, HeLa) lines; WT ARID1A re-expression suppressed both satellite and ISG expression, and ectopic SATα alone induced robust ISGs (Fig. 2F–G).
- **The phenotype is TRIM28-dependent.** TRIM28 knockdown phenocopied ARID1A loss (derepressing satellites/ERVs/ISGs), produced maximal derepression not augmented by concurrent ARID1A loss, and reversed ARID1A-overexpression–induced silencing; rescue with full-length TRIM28 but not TRIM28Δ376–624 restored repression (Fig. 3A–B).
- **The phenotype is SWI/SNF-independent.** Only 15.9% of ARID1A peaks overlapped SMARCA4-bound repeats; the SWI/SNF ATPase inhibitor BD98 and knockdown of SMARCA2/SMARCA4/SMARCAD1 induced the SWI/SNF targets ISG15/CXCL10 (and Polycomb RING1/BMI1) but left satellites, ERVs, and IFNA1 unchanged, uncoupling ARID1A's heterochromatin-guardian role from its canonical SWI/SNF function (Fig. 3C–E).
- **ARID1A preserves the TRIM28–SETDB1 interface.** ARID1A depletion did not alter bulk TRIM28, SETDB1, or HP1α protein but selectively abolished the TRIM28–SETDB1 association (HP1α intact); deleting TRIM28 aa 376–625 weakened SETDB1/HP1α recovery in ARID1A IPs, while SMARCA2/SMARCA4-deficient cells retained ARID1A interactions with SETDB1, SUV39H1, and HP1 (Fig. 4A–B).
- **ARID1A loss erodes H3K9me3/SETDB1 from repeats and opens specific loci.** On ARID1A loss, H3K9me3 and SETDB1 were eroded from repeat regions while TRIM28 peaks modestly increased (TRIM28 chromatin-bound but methyltransferase-deficient); 48.85% (2,689/5,504) of repeats losing H3K9me3/SETDB1 gained transcription; ATAC-seq showed discrete SATα/ALR and ERV (MER4D, ERVL) families became more accessible, exemplified at the MADE1 retroelement but not the euchromatic ISG15 locus (Fig. 4C–G).
- **ARID1A-low tumors are immune-active across subtypes.** In 78 colorectal cancers, ARID1A-low tumors had denser CD8+ T-cell and CD163+ M2-macrophage infiltrates; pan-cancer TCGA GSEA showed innate-immune, antigen-presentation, and T-cell signatures enriched in ARID1A-mutant tumors, most prominently in colon (COAD) and gastric (STAD) adenocarcinomas; robust innate-immune activation was seen even in MSI-high ARID1A-mutant samples (Fig. 6B–G).
- **MMR status does not gate the effect.** An isogenic MSI-like model (MLH1 knockdown in MSS HeLa) showed combined MLH1/ARID1A loss recapitulated satellite/ERV derepression and ISG induction seen with ARID1A loss alone, establishing that ARID1A deficiency amplifies antitumor immunity regardless of MMR proficiency (Fig. 6H–I).
- **Disrupting ARID1A–TRIM28 with a peptide ignites immunity in proficient tumors.** A cell-penetrating TAT-peptide spanning TRIM28 aa 376–625 quantitatively displaced ARID1A from TRIM28, derepressed satellites/ERVs/ISGs in HCT116, HeLa, and A2780 (on-target: abolished in ARID1A/TRIM28 KO and rescued only by WT, not Δ376–625, TRIM28); intratumoral peptide reduced CT26 tumor growth and elevated CD8+ T cells in the TME (Fig. 5A–H).
- **Viral mimicry drives the ARID1A-deficient phenotype.** Major-satellite-targeting LNAs and the reverse-transcriptase inhibitor lamivudine (3TC) each suppressed ISG induction in shARID1A HCT116 and slowed shARID1A CT26 tumor growth without synergizing with anti–PD-1, manifesting the role of derepressed repeats in ARID1A-mediated tumor growth (Fig. 7A–H).
- **Both DNA and RNA sensors are required.** Depletion of cGAS (MB21D1)/STING (TMEM173) or RIG-I (DDX58)/MDA5 (IFIH1) abolished ISG induction in shARID1A cells; only RIG-I/MDA5 loss enhanced clonogenic growth and reversed the in-vivo growth advantage, but the heightened anti–PD-1 sensitivity required both DNA and RNA sensing, and the surge in intratumoral CD8+ T cells was reversed by loss of either sensor (Fig. 8A–I).
- **Translational implication.** ARID1A enforces heterochromatin and suppresses viral mimicry through a SWI/SNF-independent, TRIM28-dependent axis; because global ARID1A inactivation is untenable in ARID1A-proficient tumors, discrete disruption of the ARID1A–TRIM28 interface is nominated as a druggable node to convert "cold" lesions into IFN-high, T cell–inflamed tumors and potentiate immune checkpoint therapy.

## Methods

- **ARID1A interactome:** endogenous ARID1A immunoprecipitation + Coomassie/LC-MS/MS from HCT116 cells and colon cancer patient tissue; reciprocal co-IP; deletion mapping of TRIM28 (aa 376–625) and ARID1A fragments; co-IP under SMARCA2/SMARCA4 knockdown.
- **Chromatin profiling:** ChIP-seq for ARID1A, H3K9me3, TRIM28, SETDB1 in HCT116 (GRCh38, Bowtie2, MACS2); ARID1A-dependent loci defined as peaks lost in shARID1A; RepeatMasker (hg38) repeat annotation and FPKM quantification; ATAC-seq (10x scATAC Tn5, DESeq2 differential accessibility); ChIP-qPCR (H3K4me3, CHD3 at satellites).
- **Transcriptomics:** RNA-seq of HCT116 (STAR/Cufflinks/Cuffdiff) ± ARID1A knockdown and ± peptide; GSEA (MSigDB hallmark, Reactome, GO BP); RT-qPCR of satellites (SATα, SATII, mcBOX), ERVs (MER4D, ERVL, HERVL), and ISGs (CXCL10, ISG15, IFNA1, IFNB1).
- **Genetic perturbations:** lentiCRISPRv2 sgRNAs, pLKO.1 shRNAs, siRNAs (50 nmol/L) against ARID1A, TRIM28, SMARCA2/4, SMARCAD1, MLH1, and DNA/RNA sensors (MB21D1/cGAS, TMEM173/STING, DDX58/RIG-I, IFIH1/MDA5); TRIM28-FLAG and ARID1A/TRIM28 truncation plasmids; BD98 SWI/SNF ATPase inhibitor.
- **Peptide:** cell-penetrating TAT-peptide spanning TRIM28 aa 376–625 (YGRKKRRQRRRDYNLIVIERGAAAAATGQPG) vs TAT-vehicle; co-IP displacement, RT-qPCR, on-target rescue in ARID1A/TRIM28 KO cells.
- **Imaging/clinical:** immunofluorescence/IHC (H3K9me3 foci, CD8, CD163/CD206); HSATII and HERVL RNA-FISH on colon cancer specimens; 78-patient colorectal cohort; scRNA-seq reanalysis of colorectal polyps (GSE201349) with Seurat/CellChat; pan-cancer TCGA (32 types) stratified by ARID1A mutation/MSI; pembrolizumab-treated CRC cohort (GSE179351).
- **In vivo:** subcutaneous and orthotopic CT26 and 4T1 syngeneic BALB/c models; intratumoral peptide or major-satellite LNA; 3TC (lamivudine) and anti–PD-1; ARID1A and ARID1A/sensor double-knockdown tumors; bioluminescence imaging. Data: GEO GSE239727 (RNA-seq), GSE239728 (ChIP-seq), GSE239642 (ATAC-seq).

## Open Questions

- **What distinguishes ARID1A-bound repeats that are derepressed from those that are not?** Only 48.85% of repeats losing H3K9me3/SETDB1 gained transcription; the locus-selection logic for productive viral mimicry remains unresolved.
- **Why do RNA sensors (RIG-I/MDA5) dominate over DNA sensors for clonogenic fitness while both are needed for anti–PD-1 sensitivity?** The authors note "the possible involvement of factors beyond viral mimicry in ARID1A-mediated PD-1 sensitivity," leaving the full effector hierarchy open.
- **How does ARID1A mechanically tether SETDB1 onto TRIM28?** ARID1A loss dislodges SETDB1 without changing bulk levels, but the structural/post-translational basis of the ARID1A–TRIM28–SETDB1 interface is undefined.
- **Can the TRIM28 aa 376–625 interface be drugged with a small molecule?** The proof-of-concept peptide works intratumorally; optimization of small-molecule mimetics and rational pairing with chemo-/chemoimmunotherapy is stated as the next translational step.
- **How does this pathway relate to the ARID1B–BRD4 axis and the ARID1A–R-loop–STING axis reported by others?** The authors position theirs as a separable TRIM28-tethering pathway, but the interplay among these ARID1A-linked immunogenicity routes is untested here.
