# Proteogenomics of Non-smoking Lung Cancer in East Asia Delineates Molecular Signatures of Pathogenesis and Progression.

_Cell, 2020._ PMID: [32649875](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32649875/).

DOI: [10.1016/j.cell.2020.06.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.06.012)

## Summary

Lung cancer in East Asia is characterized by a high percentage of never-smokers, early onset and predominant EGFR mutations. To illuminate the molecular phenotype of this demographically distinct disease, we performed a deep comprehensive proteogenomic study on a prospectively collected cohort in Taiwan, representing early stage, predominantly female, non-smoking lung adenocarcinoma. Integrated genomic, proteomic, and phosphoproteomic analysis delineated the demographically distinct molecular attributes and hallmarks of tumor progression. Mutational signature analysis revealed age- and gender-related mutagenesis mechanisms, characterized by high prevalence of APOBEC mutational signature in younger females and over-representation of environmental carcinogen-like mutational signatures in older females. A proteomics-informed classification distinguished the clinical characteristics of early stage patients with EGFR mutations. Furthermore, integrated protein network analysis revealed the cellular remodeling underpinning clinical trajectories and nominated candidate biomarkers for patient stratification and therapeutic intervention. This multi-omic molecular architecture may help develop strategies for management of early stage never-smoker lung adenocarcinoma.

## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

- Cohort / scope: Taiwanese early-stage, predominantly female, non-smoking lung adenocarcinoma cohort with paired tumor and adjacent normal tissues.
- Proteome: more than 10,000 unique proteins were quantified; Methods report 9,072 proteins quantified in at least 80% of patients and 6,997 proteins used for QTL mapping.
- Phosphoproteome: more than 20,000 phosphosites were quantified; Methods report 3,505 phosphopeptides commonly identified across phosphoproteomics experiments for cross-batch correlation.
- Acetylome: not reported as a separate acetylome layer in the extracted PDF text.
- MS method: TMT 10-plex proteomic and phosphoproteomic profiling, high-pH RPLC fractionation, home-made IMAC StageTip phosphopeptide enrichment.
- Instrument / platform: UltiMate 3000 RSLCnano coupled to Q Exactive HF for proteome; UltiMate 3000 RSLCnano coupled to Orbitrap Fusion Lumos for phosphoproteome.
- Software / search: Proteome Discoverer 2.1, MS Amanda for phosphoproteome, ptmRS phosphosite localization, 1% peptide/protein FDR.
- Extraction evidence: local PDF Results report >10,000 proteins and >20,000 phosphosites; STAR Methods report TMT 10-plex design, IMAC enrichment, Q Exactive HF, Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, Proteome Discoverer, MS Amanda, and ptmRS.
