# Integrated pharmaco-proteogenomics defines two subgroups in isocitrate dehydrogenase wild-type glioblastoma with prognostic and therapeutic opportunities.

_Nature communications, 2020._ PMID: [32612121](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32612121/).

DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-17139-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17139-y)

## Summary

This study combines TMT proteomics/phosphoproteomics with genomics and patient-derived-cell drug-response data to define proteomic subgroups of IDH-wild-type glioblastoma.

## Multi-Omics Identification Extraction

- Cohort/scope: 50 tumor samples and 4 normal brain tissues measured across 11 TMT sets.
- Proteome: 9,367 protein groups identified from 179,234 stripped peptides and 2,750,407 PSMs; 3,909 protein groups quantified in all global internal standards were selected for downstream analysis.
- Phosphoproteome: 8,019 phosphorylation sites and 16,377 phosphorylated peptides identified; 4,489 phosphosites quantified in three or more global internal standards were selected for downstream analysis.
- Acetylome: not reported as a separate acetylome layer in the local PDF; N-terminal acetylation was only a variable database-search modification.
- MS method: six-plex TMT isobaric labeling; high-pH peptide fractionation; phosphopeptide enrichment; LC-MS/MS; MaxQuant/Andromeda search; 1% FDR.
- Instrument/platform: Eksigent nanoLC-ultra 1D plus system coupled to Q Exactive mass spectrometer.
- Extraction evidence: local PDF Results and Methods report 9,367 protein groups, 8,019 phosphorylation sites, 16,377 phosphorylated peptides, TMT six-plex, Q Exactive, and MaxQuant.
- Interpretation note: use raw identification counts for scale comparison and selected all-GIS counts as downstream-analysis caveats.

## Key Points

- Proteomic subtyping separates IDH-wild-type GBM into groups with distinct metabolic and drug-response features.
- Phosphoproteomics supports drug-response association analysis in patient-derived cells.
- This study contributes proteome and phosphoproteome count/method/instrument data, but no acetylome dataset.
