# Targeting age-related LINE-1 activation alleviates cardiac aging

_Nature Aging, 2026._

## Summary

This study establishes the LINE-1 retrotransposon as a driver of cardiac aging acting through the cGAS–STING innate-immune pathway. The authors first show that LINE-1 ORF1p and ORF2p (protein and transcript) rise progressively in mouse hearts with age, with significant increases from 50 weeks onward. To test causality, they generate a cardiomyocyte-specific Mov10 knockout (Mov10-cKO; Myh6-Cre × Mov10^loxP/loxP), removing the MOV10 RNA helicase that normally suppresses LINE-1 retrotransposition. Mov10-cKO mice develop LINE-1 derepression (increased LINE-1 DNA content, upregulated young L1Md subfamilies L1MdA/L1MdGf/L1MdTf, elevated ORF1p/ORF2p) plus cardiac dysfunction (systolic + diastolic), cardiomyocyte hypertrophy and interstitial fibrosis, premature cardiac aging (SASP induction, SA-β-gal, p21/p16) by 3 months of age — all accompanied by cGAS–STING activation (cGAS, STING, p-TBK1, p-IRF3, p-NF-κB(p65)), type-I interferon (Ifna/Ifnb), proinflammatory ISGs, IL-1β/IL-6/TNF, and F4/80+ macrophage infiltration. In Mov10-knockout H9C2 cardiomyoblasts, genetic LINE-1 silencing (shLINE-1), pharmacological LINE-1 reverse-transcriptase inhibition (3TC) or STING inhibition (H-151) each suppressed cGAS–STING/IFN signaling and reversed senescence. Critically, in naturally aged mice, treating from 48 to 72 weeks with 3TC or H-151 improved cardiac function and reduced inflammation and senescence, with 3TC (but not H-151) also lowering LINE-1 itself — placing STING downstream of LINE-1. The work positions the LINE-1–cGAS–STING axis as a unifying mechanism integrating retrotransposon dysregulation with innate-immune activation in the aging heart, and proposes small-molecule LINE-1 / STING inhibitors as a therapeutic strategy.

## Key Points

- **LINE-1 increases in the heart with age.** Immunoblotting of C57BL/6J hearts across 10–70 weeks showed progressive increases in LINE-1 ORF1p and ORF2p, statistically significant from 50 weeks onward versus 10-week hearts; immunofluorescence confirmed more ORF1p+/ORF2p+ cardiomyocytes and higher signal intensity at 70 vs 10 weeks, and LINE-1 ORF1/ORF2 mRNA rose with age (SINEs and ERVs showing a similar but less pronounced trend).
- **MOV10 is the suppressor knocked out.** MOV10, a 5′→3′ RNA helicase, binds LINE-1 RNA to promote its degradation, disrupts ORF1p ribonucleoprotein assembly, and recruits the decapping enzyme DCP2 via phase separation. Cardiomyocyte-specific deletion (Myh6-Cre; floxed Mov10 exons 6–7) significantly reduced Mov10 mRNA and MOV10 protein at 3 months.
- **Mov10-cKO causes LINE-1 derepression.** Mov10-cKO cardiomyocytes showed significantly increased LINE-1 DNA content, upregulation of the most recently evolved LINE-1 subfamilies (L1MdA, L1MdGf, L1MdTf), and elevated ORF1p/ORF2p protein. SINEs were concomitantly altered (covarying with LINE-1) while ERVs were largely unchanged.
- **Mov10-cKO causes cardiac dysfunction at 3 months.** Increased heart/body-weight ratio, thickened ventricular walls, increased LVESD, decreased LVFS and LVEF (systolic dysfunction), decreased E/A and increased E/e′ (diastolic dysfunction), enlarged cardiomyocyte cross-sectional area (WGA), and interstitial fibrosis (Picrosirius red, Masson's trichrome).
- **Mov10-cKO drives premature cardiac aging transcriptionally.** RNA-seq found 883 up / 855 down genes; upregulated senescence genes Cdkn1a, Gdf15, Mmp12, Spp1, Tnf, Cgas; downregulated contractile genes Adra1b, Ctnna3, Dmd, Kcnj5, Myl1. Top upregulated GO terms = innate immune response; top downregulated = fatty-acid metabolism; GSEA enriched senescence pathways (NES 1.48, P = 0.0149). SASP qPCR, SA-β-gal, p21 and p16 all elevated; Cdkn1a/Cdkn2a up and Lmnb1 down.
- **LINE-1 silencing rescues senescence in cells.** Three CRISPR-Cas9 Mov10-KO H9C2 lines showed LINE-1 derepression and elevated SA-β-gal/p21/p16; lentiviral shLINE-1 silenced LINE-1 and significantly attenuated these senescence increases — direct genetic evidence that LINE-1 activation drives the aging phenotype downstream of Mov10 loss.
- **Mov10-cKO triggers cGAS–STING activation.** Increased ORF1p+cGAS+ and ORF2p+cGAS+ cardiomyocytes; immunoblot upregulation of cGAS and STING with increased phosphorylation of TBK1, IRF3 and NF-κB (p65).
- **Downstream interferon/ISG and inflammation.** qPCR showed marked upregulation of Ifna and Ifnb and multiple proinflammatory ISGs (higher ISG score); immunohistochemistry confirmed elevated IL-1β, IL-6 and TNF and enhanced F4/80+ macrophage infiltration in Mov10-cKO hearts.
- **3TC and H-151 suppress cGAS–STING in Mov10-KO cells.** LINE-1 ORF1/ORF2 transcripts were elevated in all Mov10-KO lines; 3TC (reverse-transcriptase inhibitor) reduced LINE-1 transcripts whereas H-151 (STING inhibitor) did not, as expected for a downstream target. Both inhibitors suppressed Ifna/Ifnb upregulation, reversed SASP induction and SASP score, normalized Cdkn1a/Cdkn2a/Lmnb1, and attenuated SA-β-gal — showing the axis can be blocked at either LINE-1 or STING.
- **cGAS–STING activation coincides with LINE-1 in aged hearts.** ORF1p+cGAS+ and ORF2p+cGAS+ cardiomyocytes and ORF1p/ORF2p intensity were significantly increased at 48 weeks vs 10 weeks. MOV10 also rose with age in parallel with LINE-1, interpreted as an inadequate compensatory response.
- **Pharmacological inhibition improves cardiac function in naturally aged mice.** 48-week-old mice treated for 24 weeks with 3TC (drinking water) or H-151 (i.p.) and assessed at 72 weeks showed improved LVEF/LVFS and normalized E/A and E/e′, reduced cardiomyocyte enlargement (WGA) and reduced fibrosis (Picrosirius red, Masson's trichrome); no detectable damage to liver, spleen, lung, kidney or intestine.
- **3TC lowers LINE-1 itself; both inhibitors lower downstream signaling.** Age-related increases in LINE-1 DNA content and ORF1/ORF2 transcript/protein were reversed by 3TC but not H-151. Age-associated phosphorylation of TBK1/IRF3/p65, Ifna/Ifnb, IL-1β/IL-6/TNF, F4/80+ macrophages, SA-β-gal, SASP factors/score and p21/p16 were all attenuated by both 3TC and H-151.
- **Effect generalizes to pathological aging.** 3TC given to Mov10-cKO mice from 7 weeks for 6 weeks improved systolic/diastolic function, reduced fibrosis, lowered ORF1p/ORF2p, p21/p16, SA-β-gal and IL-1β/IL-6/TNF — confirming the LINE-1–cGAS–STING–senescence axis is operational in genetically driven cardiac aging.
- **Therapeutic model.** LINE-1 derepression → cytosolic-DNA sensing via cGAS–STING → type-I IFN/SASP → chronic inflammation and cardiomyocyte senescence → cardiac dysfunction; targeting either LINE-1 reverse transcription (3TC) or STING (H-151) breaks this loop and alleviates cardiac aging.

## Methods

- **Cardiomyocyte-specific Mov10 knockout**: Mov10^loxP/loxP (exons 6–7 floxed) × Myh6-Cre; littermate Mov10^loxP/loxP controls; both sexes; genotyping by PCR.
- **Aging cohorts**: C57BL/6J mice across 10–72 weeks; LINE-1 protein/transcript profiling by immunoblot, immunofluorescence and qPCR.
- **LINE-1 readouts**: LINE-1 DNA abundance by qPCR (normalized to 5S rRNA); subfamily transcripts (L1MdA, L1MdGf, L1MdTf); ORF1p/ORF2p immunoblot and immunofluorescence (Abcam Ab216324, Ab263071).
- **Cardiac phenotyping**: echocardiography (Vevo 3100, M-mode for LVEF/LVFS/LVEDD/LVESD; PWD for E/A; tissue Doppler for E/e′); HW/BW; H&E four-chamber; WGA cross-sectional area; Picrosirius red and Masson's trichrome fibrosis.
- **Transcriptomics**: bulk RNA-seq (Illumina, DESeq2; |log2FC| ≥ 0.58, P ≤ 0.05); PCA, volcano, GO enrichment (clusterProfiler), GSEA. Accession PRJNA1338093.
- **Senescence / inflammation**: SASP and ISG qPCR panels and gene-set scores; SA-β-gal; p21/p16 immunoblot and IHC; IHC for IL-1β, IL-6, TNF, F4/80; cGAS–STING immunoblot (cGAS, STING, TBK1/p-TBK1, IRF3/p-IRF3, p65/p-p65).
- **Cell models**: three CRISPR-Cas9 Mov10-KO H9C2 lines (sgRNA in pX330-GFP); lentiviral shLINE-1 vs shCtrl knockdown.
- **Pharmacology**: 3TC (lamivudine, reverse-transcriptase inhibitor) — 10 μM 1 week in cells; 2 mg/ml drinking water in mice. H-151 (STING inhibitor) — 0.5 μM 2 weeks in cells; 750 nmol/day i.p. 5×/week in mice. Aging intervention 48→72 weeks (24 weeks); Mov10-cKO intervention 7 weeks for 6 weeks; non-cardiac organ safety by histology and serum ALT/AST/creatinine/BUN.

## Open Questions

- **Why does MOV10 rise with age yet fail to restrain LINE-1?** The authors interpret the parallel age-related MOV10 increase as an "inadequate compensatory response," but the mechanism of this failure (helicase saturation, localization, post-translational regulation) is unresolved.
- **Do non-LINE-1 transposable elements contribute?** SINEs covary with LINE-1 and rise with age; ERVs rise modestly but are unaffected by 3TC. Whether SINE/ERV derepression contributes through parallel or intersecting pathways, and whether the breakdown is a coordinated epigenetic event, is open.
- **Can the LINE-1–cGAS–STING axis be targeted to reverse, not just slow, established cardiac aging?** Intervention started at 48 weeks; whether starting later in older animals sustains or reverses established phenotypes is untested.
- **Long-term safety and translatability.** 24-week 3TC/H-151 showed no detectable non-cardiac organ damage, but long-term safety and efficacy across diverse cardiac-aging and disease models remain to be established.
