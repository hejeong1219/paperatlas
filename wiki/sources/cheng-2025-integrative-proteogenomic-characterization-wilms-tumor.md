# Integrative proteogenomic characterization of Wilms tumor.

_Nature communications, 2025._ PMID: [40830093](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40830093/).

DOI: [10.1038/s41467-025-62234-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62234-7)

## Summary

Wilms tumor (WT), the most common pediatric renal malignancy, exhibits a relatively low mutational burden compared to adult cancers, which hinders the development of targeted therapies. To elucidate the molecular landscape of WT, we perform integrative proteomic, phosphoproteomic, transcriptomic, and whole-exome sequencing analyses of WT and normal kidney tissue adjacent to tumor. Our multi-omics approach uncovers prognostic genetic alterations, distinct molecular subgroups, immune microenvironment features, and potential biomarkers and therapeutic targets. Proteome- and transcriptome-based stratification identifies three molecular subgroups with unique signatures, correlating with different histopathological subtypes and putative cellular origins at different stages of embryonic kidney development. Notably, we identify EHMT2 as a promising prognostic biomarker and therapeutic target associated with epigenetic regulation and Wnt/β-catenin pathway. In this work, we provide a comprehensive molecular characterization of WT, offering valuable insights into its pathogenesis and a foundational resource for future therapeutic development.

## Key Points

- Cohort design: 96 pediatric Wilms tumor (WT) patients; 91 WT tumors and 74 normal-adjacent-to-tumor (NAT) kidney samples profiled with multi-omics including WES, RNA-seq, proteome, and phosphoproteome.
- Multi-omics tumor–NAT signal: identifies thousands of differentially expressed genes/proteins, and retains 9,343 phosphorylation sites (after QC) for downstream phosphoproteome analyses; WT shows substantially remodeled phosphoprotein programs vs NAT.
- Integrative stratification: transcriptome/proteome-informed subgrouping separates WT into molecular subgroups associated with histopathologic subtypes and inferred developmental origins.
- Candidate biomarker/target emphasis: highlights EHMT2 as a prognostic biomarker and potential therapeutic target, discussed in the context of epigenetic regulation and Wnt/β-catenin signaling.
- Immune microenvironment: reports immune-feature differences across WT strata, positioning the dataset as a resource for linking (epi)genetic events to protein/phosphoprotein state and immune context.

## Cancer Multiomics Project Relevance

- Cancer Multiomics 과제의 “WGS(또는 WES) 변이 → 단백체/인산화단백체 기능 상태” 연결을 **tumor–NAT 비교 + subgroup stratification**으로 수행한 케이스로 참고할 수 있다.
- WT 자체는 성인 고형암과 치료 맥락이 다르지만, **저변이(낮은 TMB) 환경에서** phosphoproteome이 phenotype/stratification에 기여하는 분석 흐름(정의→QC→site-level 요약→subgroup)을 확인하는 데 유용하다.
- 실제 Cancer Multiomics 코호트에서 WGS 기반 patient stratification과 kinase signaling(인산화단백체) 연결을 설계할 때, “subgroup 정의(omics clustering) → pathway/kinase 해석 → biomarker 후보”의 구조적 템플릿으로 재사용 가능하다.
